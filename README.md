### 👋 About Me

**Md. Tasneem Al-Huzaifa Miraj** — Full-Stack Web Developer and student of Arabic Language & Literature at **Islamic University, Kushtia**, Bangladesh.

Born in Barisal, I completed my early education in traditional madrasas (Darus Sunnat Kamil Madrasa and N.S. Kamil Madrasa, Jhalakathi) before pursuing higher studies in Arabic. My passion lies in **bridging Islamic knowledge with modern technology** to serve the Ummah.

I develop clean, scalable web applications with a focus on creating impactful Islamic platforms, da'wah tools, and educational systems.

**Current Focus**: Building meaningful digital solutions that combine faith-driven purpose with professional engineering standards.
